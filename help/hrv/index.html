Heart rate variability

What HRV means in GSTS and why iPhone and Android show different numbers.

Heart rate variability (HRV) is how much the time between your heartbeats changes. GSTS compares it only with your own history, never with other people.

Your phone stores one of two different measures. They can't be compared or converted, so GSTS always names the one it shows and never mixes them.

On iPhone

Apple Health keeps HRV as SDNN, measured by your Apple Watch, mostly at rest and during sleep.

On Android

Health Connect keeps HRV as RMSSD, from the watch or ring that writes it. Not every device does.

Good to know

  • A higher or lower value than usual is only a hint about your day. Look at how you feel too.
  • If you change watch, your new values start a new history.
  • GSTS is not a medical device. It helps you understand your training, not your health.
