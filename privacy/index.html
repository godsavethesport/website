Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026.

Who we are

God Save the Sport (GSTS) is the data controller for this website and for the GSTS apps. Contact: support@godsavethesport.com.

The GSTS app

  • The app reads your workouts and health measures from Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android), only after you allow it, and only the types listed in the app. It never writes to them or changes your data there.
  • It calculates your figures on your phone. Your health data is never sent to us or to anyone else: there's no account, no server and no analytics in the app.
  • For each workout, the app shows which app and which device model recorded it. It never reads your device's name.
  • Scanning a training sheet: if you take a picture or choose a photo, the app reads the text on your phone. The picture is never saved and never sent; you check every line before anything is saved.
  • When you export your data, the file is created on your phone; where it goes is your choice.
  • Your health data is never used for advertising, never sold and never shared.

Purchases

GSTS Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google process the payment under their own privacy policies; we receive no payment details, only the confirmation that the purchase is valid, checked on your phone. We keep the stores' sales reports (country, amount, no names) as tax law requires.

Store statistics and crash reports

Apple and Google may give us aggregated statistics (downloads, countries) and crash reports, only if you allowed sharing them with app developers in your phone's settings. They contain no health data.

Beta testing

If you test GSTS before release through Apple TestFlight or a Google Play test, Apple or Google give us your name and email (if we invited you by email), your device model and crash reports. If you send feedback with a screenshot, it may show your health figures: crop it if you prefer. We use this only to run the test (you asked to take part) and delete it 90 days after the test ends.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no fonts or scripts loaded from other companies.
  • The site is hosted by GitHub (United States). GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security, under its own privacy statement; data goes to the US under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Our reason is our legitimate interest in running a safe website (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)).

When you write to us

Mail to support@godsavethesport.com is forwarded by our domain provider, Porkbun, to an inbox hosted by Google (both in the United States). We use your message only to answer you (our legitimate interest) and delete it 12 months after our last exchange. Please don't send health details you don't want us to keep.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you can ask us for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and to object, and withdraw any consent at any time. Because the app keeps your data only on your phone, you control it directly: in the app, Settings, "Delete everything on this phone" removes everything GSTS stored (your Apple Health or Health Connect data stays where it is), and deleting the app does the same. Write to support@godsavethesport.com for anything else; we answer within one month. You can complain to the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali) or to the authority where you live.

Age

GSTS is for people aged 16 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above, and the app shows the change before it applies to you.

GSTS is for training and wellbeing. It is not a medical device and gives no medical advice.
