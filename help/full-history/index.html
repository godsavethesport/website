Reading your full history

How GSTS reads your past workouts, and why Android asks once more.

GSTS reads your workouts, heart rate and sleep from your phone. Your data stays on your phone.

On iPhone

Once you allow GSTS in Apple Health, it can read your whole history.

On Android

Health Connect lets an app see only the last 30 days, unless you allow history. GSTS asks once, during setup, on See further back.

  • Allow history: GSTS reads everything Health Connect keeps.
  • Not now: GSTS starts from the first day it can read, and tells you which day that is.

To change your mind, open Settings and tap History.
