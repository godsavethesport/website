Your training on the web

Open a year, then a month, a week, a day and a workout, with the same totals at every level.

On the web, Training shows your workouts from GSTS Cloud on a big screen. It opens on this year. Choose any month, week, day or workout to see what's inside it, down to your heart rate minute by minute.

Moving around

  • Open a level: choose a bar or a row in the book. A year opens into months, a month into weeks, a week into days, a day into its workouts, and a workout into its minutes.
  • Go back: the line at the top shows where you are. Choose any step in it.
  • Previous and next: the arrows move to the month, week or day before or after.
  • One sport: choose Swims, Runs, Rides or Walks. The filter stays on as you open levels. Pace shows when one sport is chosen, since a swim's pace and a run's pace don't mix.

Every box has an i: choose it to read what the number means.

Why the numbers always add up

Each level shows the same numbers as the one above it, split up. Under the book, Adds up confirms that the rows give the total. Pace and other ratios are worked out again from the totals, never averaged.

A week that runs into the next month shows only that month's days in the month, marked with a dot. The week's own page shows all seven days.

Where the data stops

Some numbers stop at a level: resting heart rate and HRV are per day, and one minute is the finest heart rate kept. Pool swims stop at the workout until lengths sync from the phone. The page says why, under Where the data stops, instead of leaving a gap. With no maximum heart rate, training load and zone minutes have no value: set it in the GSTS app.

A coach's view

A coach sees only what you chose to share with their team, and the web enforces it. At Summary a coach sees weeks, with distance, time and workouts, and can't open a day or a workout. Your Who saw your data page lists each time they looked, as counts only. You can change the level or stop sharing at any time, in the GSTS app.

If something goes wrong

  • No training in a period: workouts appear once your phone syncs with GSTS Cloud. Try another period, or all sports.
  • GSTS isn't answering: check your connection and reload. Nothing is lost.
  • Not shared with you: the member's sharing level doesn't reach that far. Only they can change it.

The web app is for GSTS Cloud. Nothing on these pages is sent to anyone else: no tracking, no fonts or scripts from other companies.
