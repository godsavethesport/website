Your dashboard

What each tile and chart shows, and what "vs your average" means.

The first screen shows today, then the last 7 and 30 days. Every number is compared with your own recent history, never with other people.

Your sport first

Each sport has its own dashboard. Tap Swim, Run, Ride or Other at the top to change the view; Settings holds the sport GSTS opens on. Swims: when your Apple Watch records strokes, a pace is compared only with the same stroke, and mixed swimming and drills count in distance only. Runs: pace at your usual heart rate shows whether the same effort goes further; hills, heat and tiredness move it. Measurements such as sleep and resting heart rate have their own screen, the same for every sport.

The dashboard leads with the sport you picked: your last 7 days of distance, time and pace, against your previous 4 weeks. Swims show minutes per 100 m (per 100 yd in the US), runs and walks minutes per kilometre (per mile in the US and UK) and rides speed in km/h (mph in the US and UK). With "other", you see time and the number of workouts, with no pace. To change the sport, open Settings → Your sport.

The tiles

  • Exercise, steps, active energy: today's totals so far.
  • Resting heart rate: your heart rate at rest, measured by your watch. Lower than usual often goes with being well rested.
  • HRV (heart-rate variability): the small changes in time between heartbeats. Higher than usual often goes with being well rested. Values differ a lot between people: only your own trend matters.
  • Sleep: time asleep last night, with stages (core, deep, REM) if your watch records them.
  • Training load and form: see Training load and form.

Bigger charts

On iPhone, tap a chart to open it full height, with its dates and units. Drag across it to read a value. You also see your average, your highest, lowest and latest values, and you can switch between 4 weeks, 12 weeks and 1 year. The free app shows 4 weeks; GSTS Pro shows the longer ranges.

Tap a number or a title to find out what it means. Charts of measurements such as sleep and steps are in Apple Health, so GSTS doesn't enlarge those.

On Android

Switch sport with one tap on Today; Settings holds only your default sport. Measurements have their own tab. Swims show each stroke and its pace only if the recording app saves them in Health Connect; otherwise distance leads, and pace covers all strokes, rests included. Android has no strokes per 100 m or efficiency map, because Health Connect has no stroke count. Ride leads on metres climbed if saved, otherwise distance. Exercise, steps and active energy tiles come later; today you see workouts, heart rate, resting heart rate, HRV, sleep, load and form. Android measures HRV in a different way from iPhone (RMSSD instead of SDNN), so the values aren't comparable between the two phones: compare only with your own history.

"vs your average"

Under a number you may see above, below or at your average. GSTS takes the same measure on the 28 days before today, skipping days without data, and checks how far today is from that average. It needs at least 7 days with data; until then it shows nothing.

"Above" isn't good or bad by itself: a lower resting heart rate is usually better, a higher HRV usually better. The tile shows which way is good.

When numbers are missing

A tile is empty when your phone has no data of that kind yet. See Troubleshooting.
