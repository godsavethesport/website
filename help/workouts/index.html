Workouts

Your workouts by sport, and what each value means.

The Workouts screen lists your recent workouts, newest first (see Getting started for how far back). Tap one to see its details.

For every sport

  • Time, distance and energy.
  • Average and maximum heart rate.

With GSTS Pro, you also see:

  • Heart-rate zones: the minutes in each zone.
  • Load: how hard the workout was for your heart. See Training load and form.

Extra details by sport

Running form, cycling power, swim strokes and effort are in GSTS Pro. On iPhone today; on Android they come later.

  • Running: pace (minutes per km or mile), cadence (steps per minute), stride length, ground contact time, vertical oscillation and running power, when your watch measures them.
  • Cycling: speed, cadence (rpm) and power (watts), with a power meter or a watch that estimates it.
  • Swimming: pool or open water, distance, strokes and pace per 100 m.
  • Walking and other sports: time, distance, heart rate and climb.

Which values appear depends on your watch. A missing value isn't an error: your device didn't record it.

Recorded by

Under the details, Recorded by names the app and the watch or phone that saved the workout, for example "Workout · Apple Watch". GSTS reads this from Apple Health; it stays on your phone.

Lengths

With GSTS Pro, a pool swim with recorded lengths has a Lengths list: each length's number, stroke, time and, when your watch counts them, strokes. If a swim has none, see Swims without lengths.

Compare two sessions

On a workout, tap Compare with… and choose another workout of the same sport. The closest earlier one is marked Most similar: same sport and pool length, and a similar distance. GSTS shows the two side by side, the newer one first, with the difference in plain words, such as "+12 s per 100 m" or "3 strokes fewer". It never says which is better: that's for you to decide. A value only one of them has is left out, and a line under the table says which. SWOLF is shown only for swims in pools of the same length. You can compare from a plan session too, after your swim.

With only one workout of a sport, there's nothing to compare yet: GSTS compares you only with yourself.

Where is the map?

GSTS doesn't read your routes. To see where you went, open the workout in the Fitness app on iPhone, or in the app that recorded it on Android.

No workouts yet?

Start a workout in your watch's workout app. When it ends and your watch syncs, it appears here. Workouts from other apps appear too, when those apps share them with Apple Health or Health Connect.
