Write to us

Email support@godsavethesport.com. Tell us what you see, on which phone, and which version of GSTS (Settings → About). Please never send your health data or screenshots that show it unless you want to.

GSTS is made by one person, so answers can take a few days. Write in any language.

Purchases and refunds

Refunds go through the store: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google in your Google Play order history. To restore GSTS Pro on a new phone, see GSTS Pro.
