Swims without lengths

Why a swim shows only its total, with no lengths or splits.

GSTS shows lengths and splits only when your watch recorded them. It never spreads a total evenly to make them up.

Why a swim has no lengths

  • It was an open-water swim, which has no lengths.
  • The watch or app that recorded it saves only the total distance and time.
  • It was entered by hand.
  • In a pool workout, the pool length wasn't set on the watch.

What you can do

Set the pool length on your watch before you start. Your next swims will have their lengths, and you can compare them. Swims without lengths still count in your totals, your pace and your training plans.
