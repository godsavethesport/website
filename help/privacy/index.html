Privacy

What stays on your phone, what GSTS never collects, and how to delete everything.

Your data is yours. GSTS reads your health data on your phone and keeps everything there.

What GSTS does

  • Reads your workouts and health measures from Apple Health or Health Connect (the list is in Getting started).
  • Calculates your numbers on your phone.
  • Never writes to your health data and never changes it.

What GSTS never does

  • No account, no sign-in, no server: nothing is sent anywhere.
  • No advertising, no tracking, no selling or sharing of data.
  • No use of your health data except to show it to you.

Deleting your data

Delete the app: everything GSTS stored goes with it. Your original data stays in Apple Health or Health Connect, where you manage it.

Stopping GSTS from reading

On iPhone

Open Health → Sharing → Apps → GSTS and turn off what you don't want to share.

On Android

In GSTS, open Settings → Health Connect (it opens Health Connect for you), then App permissions → GSTS, and turn off what you don't want to share. Or search "Health Connect" in your phone's Settings.

The full privacy policy is at godsavethesport.com/privacy.

GSTS is for training and wellbeing. It is not a medical device and gives no medical advice.
