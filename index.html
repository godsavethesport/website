God Save the Sport

Your workouts, sleep and recovery, explained by your own history. On your phone, with no account.

Your sport, explained by your own history

GSTS reads your workouts, heart rate and sleep from Apple Health or Health Connect and shows what they mean for you: every number is compared with your own average, never with someone else's.

Human first

Sport is effort, sweat and rest of a real person. GSTS is built around that: plain words, your own numbers, and technology that serves you, not the other way round.

Private by design

Everything stays on your phone. There's no account, no advertising and no tracking. Read Privacy policy.

Free, with GSTS Pro once

The app is free: today's numbers, your last 30 days and export of your data. GSTS Pro is a one-time purchase for your full history, advanced training charts and sport details. No subscription.

Coming soon

GSTS for iPhone and Android is in testing. Questions? See Support.

GSTS is for training and wellbeing. It is not a medical device and gives no medical advice.
