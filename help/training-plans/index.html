Your training plans

Save workouts, scan a sheet, build plans, send them to Apple Watch and compare with what you swam.

Build your own weeks of training in GSTS, follow them day by day, and take a session to the pool on paper. Your plans stay on your phone, with no account.

Building plans and ticking sessions is free. Printing a PDF and sending a session to Apple Watch are part of GSTS Pro.

Build a plan

  1. Open Plans, then tap Build a plan.
  2. Give it a title, choose your sport and set the number of weeks. A new plan has 4 weeks and starts next Monday.
  3. In a week, tap Add session. Choose the day, the sport and the effort.
  4. Add the sets with Add set: how many repeats, how far or how long, and the rest after each repeat. If you don't want sets, type one distance or time under Target.
  5. Tap Save.

Repeating a week? Tap Copy to next week instead of typing it again. A week with no sessions is a rest week.

Choose the start

Open the plan and change Starts. Day 1 of week 1 falls on that date, and every other session follows from it.

Ticks

GSTS ticks a session when a workout of the same sport starts on that session's day. If you have two sessions of the same sport on one day, the longer workout ticks the longer session. A walk doesn't tick a run.

You can tick or untick a session yourself by tapping its circle, and GSTS keeps your choice. GSTS can only tick a session from workouts it has read from Apple Health, so a workout that hasn't reached your iPhone yet won't count until it does.

Print for the pool

Tap the printer next to a week, or open a session and tap Print. You see the page first, in large black type on white, with one set on each line. Tap the share button to print it or save it. Slip it into a plastic sleeve and it will last the session.

The page holds only the plan: no dates, no ticks and nothing from Apple Health.

Saved workouts

A workout is one session: warm-up, sets, cool-down. Save as many as you like and use them again.

  1. Open Plans, then Workouts, and tap New workout, or scan a sheet (below).
  2. Add the sets: how many repeats, how far or how long, and the rest after each.
  3. From a saved workout you can Print it, Send to Apple Watch, or Add to a plan, choosing the plan, the week and the day.

A plan keeps its own copy. If you change a saved workout later, plans you are already following stay as they are, with their ticks.

Scan a sheet

Got your workout on paper? Take a picture of it, or choose a photo, and GSTS reads it on your iPhone.

  1. Tap Scan a sheet.
  2. Check every line. Anything GSTS isn't sure about is marked Check this: choose what it means, or correct it.
  3. If the sheet has days, confirm the week it starts. If not, choose Today, Pick a day or Just keep it.
  4. Tap Save.

GSTS never keeps the picture: once your workout is saved, the picture is gone. Printed sheets read best. Common shorthand works, such as `8 x 100 @ 1:50`, `r20`, `k` for kick and `WU` for warm-up. A leaving time like `@ 1:50` is kept as a note for now.

Scanning is free.

Your coming days on Apple Watch

With GSTS Pro, turn on Coming days on Apple Watch and the workouts of the next 7 days appear in the Workout app on your watch by themselves. Change one on your iPhone and the watch follows.

For a workout with no day, open it and tap Send to Apple Watch.

On the watch, every step ends by itself, so you never need to tap during a swim. Your watch shows a simpler version than your sheet: keep the sheet for the details.

After your swim

Open the session to see what you planned next to what your watch measured, with any difference marked. What your watch measured always counts, and GSTS never changes it.

Then decide: Leave it as it is, or Change my plan to match. Either way the session counts as done. Add a note if you want, such as "the watch missed my kick lengths".

Good to know

  • A plan holds your targets and your own notes, never heart rates, weights or other measurements.
  • When an update needs to change your plans, GSTS asks first and keeps a copy. If something can't be read, GSTS tells you and keeps it.
  • Plans are part of your iPhone's backup, so a new iPhone restored from that backup keeps them. Plans aren't shared as files.
  • Deleting a plan removes its sessions and ticks from this iPhone. Your workouts stay in Apple Health.
