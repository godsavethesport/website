Connect your phone to your PC (testers)

Send your workouts from the phone to GSTS on your own PC over your home Wi-Fi, by scanning a QR code.

This is for testers who run GSTS on their own PC. Your workouts go from the phone to that PC and nowhere else.

Connect

  1. On the PC, open GSTS in the web app and go to Account. Under "Connect your phone to this PC", press "Show the QR".
  2. Put the phone on the same Wi-Fi as the PC.
  3. In the GSTS app, choose "Connect to my PC" and scan the QR.

The code in the QR works once and ends after 5 minutes. If it fails, make a new one on the PC.

Is it safe

The PC makes its own certificate, and the QR carries its fingerprint, so the phone only talks to your PC. The phone can send workouts but can't read anything back. It shows in Devices on the Account page, and "Sign out" there removes it.

Delete everything

"Delete everything on this PC" on the Account page erases your account and every workout stored on the PC, at once.
