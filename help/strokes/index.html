Strokes in your swims

Stroke counts, stroke types and why some swims have no stroke column.

How much GSTS can tell about your strokes depends on what your watch records and what your phone keeps.

On iPhone

Apple Watch counts your strokes in pool swims, and GSTS shows them per length, with your SWOLF (time plus strokes for a length; lower is more efficient).

On Android

Health Connect keeps which stroke you swam in each part of a workout, but not how many strokes. So GSTS shows your stroke mix and your lengths, without a strokes column. Strokes per length can't be added on Android today.

Good to know

  • Kick sets and drills are often counted as a different stroke, or not at all. That's the watch, not your swim.
  • What your watch measured always counts. GSTS never changes it.
