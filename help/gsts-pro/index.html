GSTS Pro

What Pro adds, how to buy it once, and how to restore it on a new phone.

GSTS is free. GSTS Pro is a one-time purchase: you pay once and keep it, with no subscription.

What Pro adds

  • Full history instead of the last 30 days.
  • Advanced training numbers: fitness, fatigue and form over time (see Training load and form).
  • Workout details: load and heart-rate zones, running form, cycling power, swim strokes and effort.
  • Plans at the pool: print a session or a week as a PDF, or send a session to your Apple Watch.
  • Export of your full history; the free app exports the last 30 days (see Export your data).

Buying

Tap GSTS Pro in Settings or on any locked feature, then follow your store's payment screen. The price is shown in your currency before you pay.

New phone or reinstall

Open Settings → GSTS Pro → Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple account (iPhone) or Google account (Android) you bought with. A purchase on iPhone doesn't carry over to Android, or the other way round.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by the store: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google in your Google Play order history.
